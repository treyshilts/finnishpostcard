F.P. Video Scheduler

F.P. Video Scheduler is a private video publishing and scheduling tool developed for Finnish Postcard, an independent music project based in Los Angeles.

The application allows authorized users to upload videos, organize publishing schedules, and distribute video content to social media platforms.

YouTube Integration

F.P. Video Scheduler uses the YouTube Data API Services to upload and schedule videos on authorized YouTube channels.

Users must explicitly authorize access to their YouTube account through Google's OAuth system. Videos are uploaded or scheduled only at the direction of an authorized user.

Access

F.P. Video Scheduler is intended for internal use by Finnish Postcard and is not a publicly available service.

Terms of Use

Use of this application is limited to authorized users. By using its YouTube integration, users also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service .
