Privacy Policy

F.P. Video Scheduler
Last updated: October 23, 2026

F.P. Video Scheduler is a private video publishing application operated by Finnish Postcard. This policy explains how the application handles user information and data obtained through connected services.

1. Information We Access

When an authorized user connects a Google account, F.P. Video Scheduler uses Google's OAuth system to request permission to upload videos to YouTube.

Depending on the permissions granted, the application may process:

  • Google OAuth authorization tokens
  • YouTube channel information needed for publishing
  • Videos selected for upload
  • Video titles, descriptions, and publishing settings
  • Scheduled publishing dates and times
  • Upload results and error information

F.P. Video Scheduler does not request or store Google account passwords.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate authorized users, store video uploads, manage publishing schedules, upload videos to connected platforms, and troubleshoot publishing errors.

F.P. Video Scheduler uses YouTube API Services to perform actions specifically authorized by the user. It does not independently publish videos without a user-configured publishing action or schedule.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Application data, including uploaded videos and scheduling information, may be stored using Cloudflare infrastructure. Google authorization credentials are handled through access-controlled application infrastructure.

Video content and associated publishing metadata are transmitted to YouTube when the user authorizes a YouTube upload or scheduled publication. TikTok publishing may use Buffer as a third-party publishing service.

User information is not sold to advertisers. This application is not intended to serve third-party advertising or use advertising trackers. Hosting and authentication providers may process technical request data necessary to operate and secure the service.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Videos, publishing schedules, and related application records are retained as needed to operate the scheduler and manage publishing.

Authorized users may request deletion of stored information and disconnection of their account by contacting contact@finnishpostcard.com .

Upon revocation of Google authorization, associated authorization credentials and authorized Google API data will be removed in accordance with applicable Google and YouTube API policies.

5. Revoking Google Access

Users may revoke F.P. Video Scheduler's access to their Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Connections .

Users may also contact the application operator to request disconnection and deletion of associated stored data.

6. Third-Party Services

F.P. Video Scheduler uses YouTube API Services. Users should also review the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service .

7. Contact

For questions about privacy, account access, or data deletion, contact:
